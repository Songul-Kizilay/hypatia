# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

> "Common questions deserve clear answers."

---

# What is Hypatia?

Hypatia is a local-first, modular AI platform designed for lifelong learning, cybersecurity research, intelligent automation, robotics, and human-AI collaboration.

---

# Why is the project called Hypatia?

The project is inspired by Hypatia of Alexandria, a philosopher, mathematician, and teacher who dedicated her life to the pursuit and sharing of knowledge.

The name represents curiosity, reason, and lifelong learning.

---

# Is Hypatia a chatbot?

No.

A chatbot is only one interface.

Hypatia is an ecosystem composed of multiple agents, modules, services, and intelligent systems working together.

---

# Is Hypatia cloud-based?

Hypatia follows a **Local-First** philosophy.

Whenever practical, computation should happen on the user's own device.

Cloud services may enhance capabilities but should not be mandatory.

---

# Does Hypatia replace human decision-making?

No.

Hypatia exists to assist people, not replace them.

The final decision always belongs to the user.

---

# Why is documentation written before code?

Good architecture produces better software.

Documentation-first development helps maintain quality, consistency, and long-term scalability.

---

# Is Hypatia open source?

The project is designed with open-source development in mind.

Licensing and contribution policies may evolve over time.

---

# What technologies will Hypatia use?

The exact technology stack may evolve.

Potential technologies include:

- Python
- Ollama
- FastAPI
- PostgreSQL
- Qdrant
- Whisper
- Home Assistant
- Docker

---

# Can Hypatia work without Internet?

Many capabilities are designed to work offline.

Internet access is used only when necessary or explicitly requested.

---

# Will Hypatia support robotics?

Yes.

Robotics is a long-term goal of the project.

Future versions are expected to integrate with physical robotic systems.

---

# Can Hypatia control smart homes?

Yes.

Future versions are planned to integrate with smart home platforms while respecting privacy and user control.

---

# How will Hypatia learn?

Hypatia learns by organizing knowledge, reading documentation, supporting research, and helping users understand information.

Learning should always respect privacy and transparency.

---

# Can Hypatia be customized?

Yes.

Its modular architecture allows users and developers to extend functionality through new modules, agents, and integrations.

---

# How can I contribute?

Future contribution guidelines will be available in:

- CONTRIBUTING.md
- CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md

---

# Final Answer

Hypatia is not simply software.

It is a long-term vision for trustworthy, human-centered artificial intelligence.
