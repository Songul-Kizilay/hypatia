# Autonomy-First Product Principle

Hypatia’s product direction is not a sequence of isolated assistant buttons.
For an explicitly approved, in-scope research mission, it should carry the work
through discovery, inspection, evidence, tentative comparison, explanation and
learning without requiring a person to repeatedly press Continue.

Autonomy is earned at a narrow boundary. The original mission approval defines
the goal, allowed capabilities, selected provider/scope, disclosure destination
and one cumulative budget. Derived work may continue only while all of those
facts remain true. Replanning does not create authority, and a model/provider
configuration is never permission by itself.

The preferred recovery behavior is equally important: durable completed work is
reused after restart, while a half-finished external operation is not guessed at
or silently repeated. Hypatia should state that uncertainty plainly and stop at
the boundary rather than manufacture a conclusion, spend again, or conceal the
gap.

This principle does not permit unrestricted internet exploration, target testing,
tool execution, provider substitution or fact promotion. It guides the product
toward useful end-to-end learning while preserving explicit scope, privacy,
budget, provenance and human control.
